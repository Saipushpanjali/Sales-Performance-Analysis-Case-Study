# Sales-Performance-Analysis-Case-Study
This project focuses on analyzing sales transaction data to understand overall business performance, customer purchasing behavior, and product profitability using Excel and Power BI. The dataset consists of 1,000 sales records and includes key fields such as Order ID, Order Date, Region, Product Category, Product Name, Quantity, Sales Amount, and Profit. The primary objective of the analysis is to identify high- and low-performing regions, evaluate product categories and individual products, analyze monthly sales trends, and derive actionable business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

Several key performance indicators were calculated to support the analysis. Total Sales and Total Profit were computed as the sum of sales amount and profit respectively, while Total Orders were derived by counting unique order IDs. Average Order Value (AOV) was calculated by dividing total sales by total orders, providing insight into customer spending behavior. Profit Margin was calculated as total profit divided by total sales, helping assess overall and segment-wise profitability.

Using Excel Pivot Tables, region-wise analysis was performed to compare total sales, profit, AOV, and profit margin across East, North, South, and West regions. The analysis revealed that the East region achieved the highest profit margin, while the South region recorded the lowest. The North region stood out with the highest average order value, indicating higher-value purchases, whereas the East region had the lowest AOV. Product category analysis showed that Electronics delivered the highest profit margin, while the Home category recorded the highest AOV. Electronics also had the lowest AOV among categories, indicating high volume but lower per-order value.

Monthly sales trend analysis highlighted seasonality in sales performance. March recorded the highest sales, while July showed the lowest sales, indicating a potential period of reduced customer demand. Product-wise analysis further revealed disparities in product performance, with Kitchen Mixer emerging as the highest-selling product and Power Bank generating the lowest sales.

To complement the Excel analysis, an interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to visualize these insights dynamically. The dashboard includes KPI cards displaying Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, AOV, and Profit Margin, along with visualizations for region-wise sales, category-wise profit margins, monthly sales trends, and product-level sales distribution. Interactive slicers allow users to filter the dashboard by region, product category, and month, enabling deeper exploration of the data.

Overall, the analysis highlights strong regional and category-level performance differences, seasonal sales patterns, and uneven product contribution to revenue. Based on these findings, it is recommended to focus marketing efforts on high-AOV regions such as North, promote high-margin categories like Electronics, implement targeted campaigns during low-sales months such as July, reassess the strategy for low-performing products like Power Bank, and consider product bundling strategies to improve AOV and profitability. This project demonstrates the effective use of Excel and Power BI to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through structured analysis and clear visual storytelling.
